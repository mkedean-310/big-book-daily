Privacy Policy

AA Step Study - for Recovery · Last updated November 17, 2026

This app is a free, personal daily study of the book Alcoholics Anonymous (the “Big Book”). It is offered as a website and as an Android app that opens the same website. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

No personal data is collected

The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

Your journal stays on your device

Anything you write in the journal, along with your reading progress and display settings (such as text size and light or dark theme), is saved only on your own device, in your browser’s local storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. If you clear the app’s data, uninstall the app, or clear your browser storage, those entries are deleted from your device and cannot be recovered by us.

No ads, no tracking

The app has no advertising, no analytics, no tracking cookies, and no third-party tracking tools. We do not sell or share any data, because we do not have any.

How the app is delivered

The app’s pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) to deliver and secure the site; see GitHub’s privacy statement. The app itself does not collect or read these logs.

Children

The app is not directed to children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Not affiliated

This app is an independent personal project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or approved by Alcoholics Anonymous World Services, Inc.
